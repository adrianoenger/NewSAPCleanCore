# ADR-001 — PoC Scope and Explicit Non-Goals

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-23

## Decision
The next generation remains a PoC. Prioritize demonstrable product value and development speed. Authentication, RBAC, enterprise audit, HA/DR, advanced observability and production-grade security/scaling are out of scope.

## Consequence
Implementation and sprint acceptance criteria must remain consistent with this decision. Any future change requires updating or superseding this ADR.
